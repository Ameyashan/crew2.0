# Cofounder — market analysis (weekly)

Once a week, step back and think about where Jugaadu should go. Use the repo's
`deep-research` skill and web search to ground claims in real, cited sources —
don't speculate from memory.

Jugaadu today: 4 live agents (Resume tailor, Person-finder, Outreach drafter,
Email verifier) + 4 branded-but-unbuilt "incoming" agents (Article Publisher,
Research Briefer, Upskill Coach, Network Mapper). Freemium, beta, Google-auth,
"first 250 keep three agents free forever."

## Cover (each week; rotate depth)
1. **Competitive landscape** — AI job-hunt / auto-apply / outreach tools
   (e.g. LazyApply, Simplify, Teal, Sonara, LoopCV, Kickresume, plus new
   entrants). For 3–5: what they do, pricing, positioning, and where Jugaadu is
   differentiated or exposed. Cite sources.
2. **Market signals** — hiring-market and job-seeker trends, "hidden job market"
   / referral data, relevant regulation (auto-apply bans, ATS changes), and
   platform risk (LinkedIn/Apollo/Hunter ToS on the enrichment Jugaadu relies on).
3. **Where to go** — 2–3 concrete direction options tied to the unbuilt agents or
   new wedges, each with the bet, who it's for, the risk, and the smallest test
   to validate it this month.
4. **Pricing** — one grounded observation on what this market charges and what
   Jugaadu could sustainably charge past beta.

## Output
- A dated brief at `docs/cofounder/market/YYYY-Www.md` (ISO week), emailed in the
  weekly standup digest. Lead with a 3-bullet "if you read nothing else" summary,
  then the detail with citations. No code changes — recommendations only.
