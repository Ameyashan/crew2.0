# Growth — 2026-07-10 (first live standup)

Reality check from the data: ~2 active users, 3 signups in 5 weeks. The product
engine works (499 runs, ~0 errors); **the gap is distribution.** Goal this week:
get 10–20 *right* people to run their first job through the crew. Ideas below are
concrete and testable; drafts follow the `cold-outreach` skill + anti-AI rules.

## Channel bets (pick 2 to run this week)
1. **r/cscareerquestions + r/jobs "show, don't tell" comments.** Find threads
   where someone's stuck cold-applying with no callbacks. Reply with the *actual
   move* (find the hiring manager, message them) and a one-line "I built a tool
   that does this" — link optional. Metric: click-throughs to /home.
2. **Build-in-public on X/LinkedIn from the changelog.** You already ship a public
   changelog — that's SEO + social fuel going unused. Turn each week's shipped
   items into one short "here's what the crew learned to do this week" post.
   Metric: profile visits → signups.
3. **10 warm DMs to job-switchers in your network.** Not "check out my startup" —
   "you mentioned you're looking; want me to find the hiring manager for one role
   you care about? Takes 2 min." One ask, high value, no calendar link.

## 5 outreach targets (warm, non-spammy)
- Friends between roles (the "founder between things" cohort your marquee names).
- New grads in your network fighting the application pile.
- 1–2 career coaches / bootcamp career-services leads (they have distribution).
- An IndieHackers "what are you working on" thread.
- One relevant Slack/Discord (job-hunt or your alma mater's) — lead with help.

## Note for the founder
The highest-leverage growth act right now isn't a campaign — it's making the
**"send"** step obvious and satisfying in-product (see analytics brief: 84 drafts,
0 sends recorded). Distribution converts far better once the core loop visibly
closes.
